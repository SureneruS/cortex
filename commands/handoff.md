You are about to be rotated — a fresh session will continue your work. Export your current state comprehensively so the next session can pick up seamlessly.

Provide a structured handoff summary:

## 1. Goal

What are you working on? One sentence.

## 2. Progress

What's done so far? Bullet points.

## 3. Pending

What's left to do? Bullet points with priority.

## 4. Blockers

Any open questions, issues, or decisions needed?

## 5. Key Context

- Important files you've been working with (paths)
- Decisions made and why
- Patterns or gotchas discovered
- Any running processes, branches, or PRs

## 6. Where You Left Off

Exactly what you were doing when stopped. Enough detail to resume immediately.

Be comprehensive but concise. This summary becomes the starting prompt for your replacement session.
